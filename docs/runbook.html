Runbook

Something wrong? Find the symptom, read why it happens, and follow the taps. For version 1.1.

An opening or a trap is locked

Why
Free is any 3 openings and 3 traps. The rest is Pro.

The taps that fix it:

  1. Tap the lock to see the Pro sheet.
  2. If you already bought Pro, open Settings, See what Pro adds, and tap Restore purchase.

Get Pro does nothing, or the payment fails

Why
Pro goes through the Google Play app on your phone. It needs Play, a Google account on the phone and a working payment method.

The taps that fix it:

  1. Open the Google Play app and check you are signed in and it works.
  2. Check the payment method in Play.
  3. Close Chess Pass, open it again and tap Get Pro.
  4. If Play shows an error, note the text and write to zanoski.marko@gmail.com.

I paid, but Pro is still locked

Why
Chess Pass asks Google Play when it starts. A slow or pending payment can take a few minutes to show. The app says "The payment is pending" until Play confirms it.

The taps that fix it:

  1. Wait a few minutes, then close and reopen Chess Pass.
  2. Open Settings, See what Pro adds, and tap Restore purchase.
  3. Make sure the phone uses the Google account that paid. "There is no Pro purchase on this Google account" means it is another account.
  4. Check Play, Payments and subscriptions, Budget and history, that the payment went through.
  5. Still locked? Write to zanoski.marko@gmail.com with the date and the order number from your Google Play receipt.

I got a refund and Pro went away

Why
Chess Pass asks Google Play again when it starts, so a refund takes Pro away. That is expected.

The taps that fix it:

  1. Nothing to fix. Your progress stays. Locked openings go back to locked, and the Eval bar row leaves Settings.

Pro is gone on my new phone

Why
Pro belongs to your Google account, not to the phone.

The taps that fix it:

  1. Sign in with the same Google account.
  2. Open Settings, See what Pro adds, and tap Restore purchase.

I cannot find the eval bar

Why
The eval bar is part of Pro. It also has its own switch in Settings.

The taps that fix it:

  1. Open Settings. If there is no Eval bar row, Pro is not unlocked: tap See what Pro adds, then Get Pro or Restore purchase.
  2. If the row is there, switch Eval bar on.

My progress is gone on a new phone

Why
Progress stays only on the phone it was made on. It is kept out of cloud backups on purpose.

The taps that fix it:

  1. If you still have the old phone, open Settings, Backup there and tap Save progress to a file.
  2. Copy the file across, then open Settings, Backup on the new phone and tap Restore progress from a file.
  3. With no backup file the old progress cannot be recovered, because it was never sent anywhere.

Restore says the file is not a Chess Pass backup

Why
The file was not made by Settings, Backup, or it was changed or cut short while copying.

The taps that fix it:

  1. Copy the file across again and check its size matches.
  2. Pick the file named like the one you saved, not a screenshot or a download.
  3. If it still fails, save a fresh backup on the old phone.

I cannot give back one of my 3 free openings

Why
A pick can be given back only until you learn a line in it.

The taps that fix it:

  1. Pick a different opening for an open slot, or buy Pro to open all 52.

A widget does not appear, or the page says my launcher cannot add widgets

Why
Some launchers do not let an app pin a widget. Others need you to place it.

The taps that fix it:

  1. Touch and hold an empty spot on your home screen.
  2. Tap Widgets, then pick Chess Pass and one of Today, Next move or Streak.
  3. If the launcher asks where to put it, drop it on a free spot.

A widget shows old numbers

Why
Widgets refresh when your progress changes and when you open the app. A launcher that puts apps to sleep can hold an old picture.

The taps that fix it:

  1. Open Chess Pass once. The widgets refresh.
  2. Still old? Remove the widget and add it again from Settings, Widgets and tiles.
  3. In the phone settings, allow Chess Pass to run without battery limits.

The Next move widget says to pick an opening

Why
It shows a position from your lines, and you have none yet.

The taps that fix it:

  1. Open Openings, pick one and learn a line. The widget fills in.

The Review now tile is missing

Why
A tile must be added once. Some phones cannot add it from the app.

The taps that fix it:

  1. Open Settings, Widgets and tiles and tap Add the tile.
  2. Or swipe down twice, tap the pencil, and drag Review now into your tiles.
  3. It reads Nothing due when no line is due, which is normal.

No sound on moves

Why
Sound can be switched off in Settings.

The taps that fix it:

  1. Open Settings and switch Sound on.
  2. Check the phone is not on silent and the media volume is up.

Reminders do not show

Why
Android notifications for Chess Pass are off, or reminders paused after 3 you ignored in a row.

The taps that fix it:

  1. Open Settings, Reminders. If it says notifications are off, tap Open the phone's settings and allow them.
  2. Open Chess Pass once. Paused reminders start again.

Chess Pass is not in the share sheet, or the game does not open

Why
Chess Pass takes a game as PGN text. Some apps share a picture or a link instead.

The taps that fix it:

  1. In the other app, look for Export or Share as PGN, and share the PGN, not a link or an image.
  2. Or copy the game, open Openings and tap Paste a game.
  3. If the game still does not show where it left a line, its first moves may not match any of the 52 openings.

Today shows no lines, or too many

Why
Lines come due as their review gap runs out. The daily review cap limits how many show.

The taps that fix it:

  1. Open Settings, Daily review cap to change the most reviews a day.
  2. Learn a new line from Openings to add to the queue.

The app looks too big, too small or too dark

Why
Chess Pass follows the phone for look and text size.

The taps that fix it:

  1. Open Settings, Look and pick Light, Dark or Black.
  2. Change the text size in the phone settings, Display.

The app crashes or looks wrong

Why
Something unexpected.

The taps that fix it:

  1. Update Chess Pass in Google Play and restart the phone.
  2. Save a backup in Settings, Backup first, if you can.
  3. Report it with the steps, your app version (Settings, About), phone and Android version: Report a bug.
