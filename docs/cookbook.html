Cookbook

Cookbook

Short recipes, a few taps each. Screen names are the labels in the app. For version 1.1.

Pick your 3 free openings

  1. Open Openings.
  2. Tap an opening to read its idea.
  3. Tap Pick as a free opening.

You can tap Give this pick back until you learn a line in it.

Learn a line

  1. Open an opening and tap the key at the bottom for the next line.
  2. Watch the line play itself. Tap Pause, Back or Next to go at your pace.
  3. Tap Practice it.
  4. Play each move on the board: tap the piece, then the square.

Watch a line again

  1. Open the opening and tap the line.
  2. Tap Watch. The line plays itself with a note on every move.
  3. Tap Back to step back, Pause to stop, or Next to go on.
  4. Tap Practice it when you are ready.

Do today's review

  1. Open Today.
  2. Tap Start review.
  3. Play each line from memory.
  4. After each one tap Next line, or Done for today.

Get a hint

  1. In Practice, tap Hint. The piece that moves next lights up.
  2. Tap Hint again. The arrow and the reason for the move show.

See why a move is wrong

  1. In Practice, play a move that is not in the line.
  2. Read the sheet: The line, Your move, and the reason.
  3. Tap Show why to watch the engine line, or Back to the line to try again.

Play on from a mistake

  1. Play a move off the line.
  2. On the sheet, tap Play on.
  3. Keep playing the position. Tap Back to the line to return.

Play a line out to the end

  1. Finish a line in Learn, Review or Practice.
  2. On the end sheet, tap Play it out.
  3. Play the game on against the computer. Tap Start over to go back to move one.

Find where your game left the book

  1. In your chess app, open a finished game.
  2. Tap Share or Export and pick PGN.
  3. Choose Chess Pass in the share sheet.
  4. Read the Your game screen, then tap Drill this line today.

Or copy the game, open Openings and tap Paste a game.

Learn a trap

  1. Open Traps.
  2. Tap Play the trap to win with it, or Stop the trap to learn the safe reply.

Play a match

  1. Open Today and tap Play a match.
  2. Pick Difficulty and You play.
  3. Tap Resign to give up, or Continue your match on Today to come back to a saved one.

Look up a chess word

  1. Open Words.
  2. Type in Search words, or open a group.
  3. Tap Mark as learned when you know it.

Add a home screen widget

  1. Open Settings, Widgets and tiles.
  2. Tap the widget you want: Today, Next move or Streak.
  3. Confirm the placement in the launcher.

If the page says your launcher cannot add widgets from here: touch and hold an empty spot on your home screen, tap Widgets, then pick Chess Pass.

Add the Review now tile

  1. Open Settings, Widgets and tiles.
  2. Tap Add the tile and confirm.
  3. Swipe down twice to find Review now in Quick Settings.

If the button is missing: swipe down twice, tap the pencil, and drag Review now into your tiles.

Practise from the Next move widget

  1. Add the Next move widget.
  2. Tap it. Practice opens at that position, with you to move.

Turn the eval bar on or off

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Switch Eval bar on or off.

The row shows only when Pro is unlocked.

Change your level

  1. Open Settings, Your level.
  2. Pick the rating closest to yours.

Change how much you do each day

  1. Open Settings, Daily review cap.
  2. Pick the most reviews a day.

Set a reminder

  1. Open Settings, Reminders.
  2. Switch on When reviews are due, Daily reminder or Keep my streak, and set the time under When.
  3. Allow notifications when Android asks.

Change the look, board or pieces

  1. Open Settings, Look.
  2. Pick a Theme: Follow the phone, Light, Dark or Black.
  3. Pick a Board (Grass or Blue) and Pieces (Classic or Celtic).

Turn sound or vibration on or off

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Switch Sound or Vibration.

Buy Pro

  1. Tap any locked opening or trap, or open Settings, See what Pro adds.
  2. Tap Get Pro. Google Play takes the one payment.

It is paid once, with no subscription.

Get Pro back on a new phone

  1. Sign in to the phone with the same Google account you bought with.
  2. Open Settings, See what Pro adds.
  3. Tap Restore purchase.

Save a backup

  1. Open Settings, Backup.
  2. Tap Save progress to a file, and pick where to put it.

Move to a new phone

  1. On the old phone, save a backup, and copy the file to the new phone.
  2. Install Chess Pass on the new phone.
  3. Open Settings, Backup, Restore progress from a file and pick the file.
  4. Open Settings, See what Pro adds, Restore purchase if you bought Pro.

Progress is kept out of cloud backups on purpose, so a backup file is the way across.
