Questions and answers

What is free and what is Pro?

Free: any 3 openings you pick, with every line, plus the traps Scholar's Mate, Fried Liver and Legal's Mate. Watch, the two step hint, Play it out, practice matches, Words and the widgets are free too. Pro, 2.49 EUR once (1.99 in countries with lower prices), opens all 52 openings, all 15 traps and the eval bar.

Can I have more than 3 free openings?

No. It never grows past 3, even when all 3 are learned. A pick can be given back only until you learn a line in it.

Is it a subscription?

No. Pro is one payment through Google Play. There is nothing to cancel.

What is Watch?

A new line plays itself, slowly, with a note on every move. You can pause, step back or step on, then tap Practice it. With Pro you also see how the engine number changes on each move.

What does the hint do now?

Two steps. The first tap lights the piece that moves next. The second tap draws the arrow and gives the reason.

What is Play it out?

After a line ends, play the game on against the computer at your level to see how it goes. Start over returns you to move one. It is not offered when the line ends in checkmate.

Is the eval bar free?

No, it is part of Pro. With Pro, the Eval bar switch in Settings turns it on or off. Free users still get the verdict and the reason for an off line move.

What are the widgets?

Today (lines due, your streak and a key to start), Next move (a position from your lines, tap to practise it) and Streak (a small tile, lit once you have trained today). There is also a Quick Settings tile, Review now. Add them in Settings, Widgets and tiles. They are free and send nothing anywhere.

Which pieces can I use?

Classic, drawn for Chess Pass, and Celtic by Maurizio Monge (MIT license). Pick them in Settings, Look.

Does it work without internet?

Always. It has no internet permission. Every opening, note and number is inside the app. Only buying Pro goes through the Google Play app, and the guide and privacy policy open in your browser.

Does it collect my data?

No. No account, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting. See the privacy policy.

Where do the rankings come from?

From the Lichess open database (CC0): we counted ahead of time how often club players at each rating play each opening, and the app ships those numbers. Chess Pass is independent and not endorsed by Lichess.

How are off line moves explained without internet?

A strong engine checked every line position and every other legal move while we built the app. The app ships those results with a reason. Play on and Play it out use a small engine built into the app.

How do I share a game?

In any chess app, share the game as PGN and choose Chess Pass, or copy it and tap Paste a game in Openings. Nothing goes online. See the cookbook.

I bought Pro on my old phone.

Open Settings, See what Pro adds, and tap Restore purchase. Use the same Google account.

I moved my phone and my progress is gone.

Progress stays only on the phone where you made it, and is kept out of cloud backups on purpose. Use Settings, Backup to save a file and restore it on the new phone. See the runbook.

Will it raise my rating?

We make no promise. Chess Pass helps you remember lines and the ideas behind them. Results depend on your practice.

Which languages does it speak?

The app comes in 23 languages: English, Chinese (Simplified), Czech, Dutch, French, German, Greek, Hindi, Hungarian, Indonesian, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Polish, Portuguese (Brazil), Romanian, Russian, Spanish, Swedish, Thai, Turkish, Ukrainian and Vietnamese. It follows the phone, or pick one in Settings, Language. The notes on the moves and the chess words stay in English.

Which Android version do I need?

Android 10 or newer.

A line or a note looks wrong.

Please report it with the opening, the line and the move.
