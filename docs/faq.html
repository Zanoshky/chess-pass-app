Questions and answers

What is free and what is Pro?

Free: any 3 openings you pick, with every line, plus the traps Scholar's Mate, Fried Liver and Legal's Mate. Pro, 2.49 EUR once (1.99 in countries with lower prices), opens all 52 openings and all 15 traps.

Can I have more than 3 free openings?

No. It never grows past 3, even when all 3 are learned. A pick can be swapped only until you learn a line in it.

Is it a subscription?

No. Pro is one payment through Google Play. There is nothing to cancel.

Does it work without internet?

Always. It has no internet permission. Every opening, note and number is inside the app. Only buying Pro goes through the Google Play app, and the guide and privacy policy open in your browser.

Does it collect my data?

No. No account, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting. See the privacy policy.

Where do the rankings come from?

From the Lichess open database (CC0): we counted ahead of time how often club players at each rating play each opening, and the app ships those numbers. Chess Pass is independent and not endorsed by Lichess.

How are off line moves explained without internet?

A strong engine checked every line position and every other legal move while we built the app. The app ships those results with a reason. Play on uses a small engine built into the app.

How do I share a game?

In any chess app, share the game as PGN and choose Chess Pass. Nothing goes online. See the cookbook.

I bought Pro on my old phone.

Open Settings, Pro, and tap Restore purchase. Use the same Google account.

I moved my phone and my progress is gone.

Progress stays only on the phone where you made it, and is kept out of cloud backups on purpose. Use Settings, Backup to save a file and load it on the new phone. See the runbook.

Will it raise my rating?

We make no promise. Chess Pass helps you remember lines and the ideas behind them. Results depend on your practice.

Which Android version do I need?

Android 10 or newer.

A line or a note looks wrong.

Please report it with the opening, the line and the move.
