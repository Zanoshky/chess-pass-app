How Chess Pass works

Every screen, in the order you meet them. The names here are the labels in the app. This guide is for version 1.3. New in 1.3: text sizes are simpler and easier to read.

The bottom bar

Four sections: Today, Openings, Traps and Words. Settings is the gear at the top right of every section. A drill, an opening, Pro and the Settings pages open full screen, and Back takes you out of them.

Today

The verdict first: how many lines are due today, how long they take, and which openings they come from. They are mixed, so you learn to tell openings apart. Start review begins. Under it:

  • Keep learning: the openings you are in the middle of, with which line is next.
  • Next 7 days: how many lines come due each day.
  • So far: your openings, lines known, day streak and rating.
  • Play a match: a full game against the engine, see Practice match.

With nothing due the card says All done for today, and Learn a new line adds a fresh one.

Openings

All 52 openings, most played first. The ranking is how often club players at your rating play each one. Chips at the top filter the list: All, As White, Against 1.e4, Against 1.d4 and Other. A card on top says how many free openings you have picked: any 3 are free, and the other 49 come with Pro. Tap an opening to open its page. Tap a locked one and a sheet shows what Pro adds. Share a game and Paste a game are here too, see A shared game.

An opening

Its name and tags, including Free pick if it is one of your 3. Then a board, and:

  • The idea: what the opening is trying to do, in plain English.
  • How it scores: how its games ended for White, Black and draws, and how often it is played.
  • Lines: each line, with where it stands: not learned yet, due for review today, or back in some days.

The key at the bottom starts the next line. Pick as a free opening makes it one of your 3. A pick can be given back with Give this pick back only until you have learned a line in it.

Watch

A new line opens in Watch first. The line plays itself, slowly, and every move comes with a note, yours and your opponent's. Keys: Back, Pause or Play, and Next to step by hand. At the last move the note says "That is the whole line", and Practice it starts your try.

With Pro, each move also shows how the engine number changes, as a short sentence like "Engine +0.3. About the same as before: a sound move." Small changes read as steady. These numbers come from the engine results that ship in the app.

Practice

You play the line from memory. The top shows whose turn it is (Your move), and your misses and hints. Move a piece by tapping it and then its square.

  • A right move plays and the line goes on. After your try, the note on that move says why it is played.
  • A wrong move is shown and you try again, and it counts as a miss.
  • Hint works in two steps. The first tap lights the piece that moves next and counts as one hint. The second tap draws the arrow and shows the reason for the move.
  • Start over goes back to the first move.
  • Moves lists the moves played so far.

With Pro and the Eval bar switch on, a bar beside the board and a score at the top show who is better. At the end, Line learned shows your misses and hints and when the line is back for review.

Off the line

Play a move that is not in the line and a sheet opens. It says how good or bad the move is and why, with two rows: The line and Your move, each with its score. The numbers were computed ahead of time by a strong engine and ship in the app, so this works offline. Three keys:

Back to the line
Undo your move and go on with the line.
Show why
Plays the engine line, so you can see what happens.
Play on
Keep playing the position from here against the computer at your level, with a small engine built into the app.

Play it out

When a line ends, the sheet offers Play it out (unless the line ends in checkmate). The game goes on from the last position against the computer at your level, so you see how the opening turns into a game. It works in openings and traps, in Learn, Review and Practice. Start over takes you back to move one of the line.

After a review

Each clean review pushes a line further out along the ladder: 1 day, 3 days, 1 week, 2 weeks, 1 month, 3 months. A miss steps it back. The sheet shows Clean review or Back a step, the date the line is back, and how many due lines are left today. Next line goes on, and Done for today stops.

Traps

15 traps, three of them free: Scholar's Mate, Fried Liver and Legal's Mate. A featured trap sits on top with two keys: Play the trap (win with it against a player who does not know the line) and Stop the trap (learn the safe reply when someone tries it on you: you play the other side, Watch shows the safe line first, and it comes back for review like any line). All traps lists the rest. A locked trap opens the Pro sheet.

Words

A dictionary of 152 chess words in plain English, each with a small board diagram. Search at the top, or browse by group: The board, Pieces and rules, Writing moves, Tactics, Strategy, Openings, Endgame, How games end. On a word, swipe or use Previous word and Next word, and Mark as learned when you know it. Study the rest walks the words you have not learned yet.

A shared game (Your game)

In any chess app, share a game as PGN and pick Chess Pass in the Android share sheet. Or copy the game and tap Paste a game in Openings. The moves pass on your phone and nothing goes online. The Your game screen shows the opening, which side you played, and the move where the game left your line, with a verdict. Two rows give the score of the line's move and of the move played, and a short reason follows. Drill this line today adds the line to today's review.

Practice match

On Today, Play a match lets you pick Difficulty (Easy, Normal or Hard) and You play (White, Black or Random). A finished game moves your rating. The match is saved after every move, and Today shows Continue your match if one is open. Resign counts as a loss. It is free for everyone.

Pro

Opened from Settings or from anything locked. It shows Every opening, once with the price, What you get, and Free stays free. Pro is 2.49 once (1.99 in many countries) and adds:

  • All 52 openings and all 15 traps.
  • The Eval bar: the engine score beside the board, in the drill header, after each watched move and in Check your games.
  • One review for everything: every line you learn joins one mixed daily review.

Get Pro takes the one payment through Google Play, with no subscription and no account. Restore purchase brings it back on a new phone with the same Google account. Not now closes the sheet. Free users never see the eval bar, but the verdict on the off line sheet stays free.

Settings

Open it with the gear at the top right.

Look
Theme, Board and Pieces, see below.
Language
Follow the phone, or pick one of 23 languages for the app. The notes on the moves and the chess words stay in English.
Your level
The rating closest to yours. It ranks the openings and sets how strong the computer plays in Play it out and Play on.
Eval bar
A switch, shown only when Pro is unlocked. On by default. It hides or shows the bar, the score in the drill header, the engine sentence in Watch and the bar in Check your games.
Sound
Soft move, capture and check sounds.
Vibration
A light buzz on check, stronger on mate, and its own pattern when you win or lose.
Reminders
When reviews are due, a daily reminder and Keep my streak, with the time you practise. They pause after 3 in a row you ignore.
Widgets and tiles
Add the home screen widgets and the Quick Settings tile, see below.
Daily review cap

Backup

Look

Settings, Look. Theme: Follow the phone, Light, Dark or Black (true black for OLED). Board: Grass (the default) or Blue. Pieces: Classic (the default, drawn for Chess Pass) or Celtic, carved pieces by Maurizio Monge under the MIT license. Picking one applies at once.

Widgets and tiles

Settings, Widgets and tiles shows previews. Tap a widget to add it to your home screen.

Today
Lines due and your streak. Bigger sizes add a key: Review now when lines are due, or Learn a new line when none are. Taller sizes show the next line.
Next move
A position from your lines with you to move. Tap it and Practice opens at that position.
Streak
A small 1 by 1 widget with your day streak, lit once you have trained today.
Review now
A Quick Settings tile. It reads "N lines due" or "Nothing due", and opens Chess Pass to start the review. Tap Add the tile on the same page.

If your launcher cannot add widgets from the page, touch and hold an empty spot on your home screen, tap Widgets, then pick Chess Pass. If the tile cannot be added from the page, swipe down twice, tap the pencil, and drag Review now into your tiles. The widgets read your progress on the phone and send nothing anywhere.

Backup

Your progress stays on the phone and is kept out of Android's cloud backup and out of the copy to a new phone, on purpose. Settings, Backup offers Save progress to a file and Restore progress from a file, and only you decide where the file goes. To move to a new phone, see the cookbook.

Privacy

The promise chips (no ads, no tracking, no analytics, no crash reports, no account, no AI, no internet), then the permissions: Google Play billing for Pro, vibration, notifications for the reminders you turn on, and start at boot to set them again. Chess Pass has no internet permission. The last row opens the privacy policy at chesspass.zanoski.com/privacy.
