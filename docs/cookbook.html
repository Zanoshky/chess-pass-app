Cookbook

Cookbook

Short recipes, a few taps each. Screen names are the labels in the app.

Pick your 3 free openings

  1. Open Openings.
  2. Tap an opening to read its idea.
  3. Tap the key at the bottom, like Learn line 1, to learn a line. That opening is now one of your 3.

You can swap a pick until you learn a line in it.

Learn a line

  1. Open an opening and tap the key at the bottom, like Learn line 3.
  2. Play each move on the board: tap the piece, then the square.
  3. Read the note after each move.
  4. At the end, tap Play it again, or go back.

Do today's review

  1. Open Today.
  2. Tap Start review.
  3. Play each line from memory.
  4. After each one tap Next line, or Done for today.

Get a hint

  1. In the drill, tap Hint.
  2. The move shows on the board. It counts as a hint.

See why a move is wrong

  1. In the drill, play a move that is not in the line.
  2. Read the sheet: your move, the line, and the reason.
  3. Tap Show why to watch the engine line, or Back to the line to try again.

Play on from a mistake

  1. Play a move off the line.
  2. On the sheet, tap Play on.
  3. Keep playing the position. Go back out with Back.

Find where your game left the book

  1. In your chess app, open a finished game.
  2. Tap Share or Export and pick PGN.
  3. Choose Chess Pass in the share sheet.
  4. Read the Your game screen, then tap Drill this line today.

Learn a trap

  1. Open Traps.
  2. Tap Set it to play the trap, or Dodge it to learn to see it coming.

Change how much you do each day

  1. Open Settings, Daily practice.
  2. Set the number of new lines and the most reviews.

Change the review order

  1. Open Settings, Review order.
  2. Pick the order you want. The default is Mixed openings.

Change the look or the board

  1. Open Settings, Look to pick Light, Dark or Black.
  2. Open Board and pieces for the board, pieces and coordinates.
  3. Open Moves and sound for legal moves and sound.

Buy Pro

  1. Tap any locked opening or trap, or open Settings, Pro.
  2. Tap Get Pro. Google Play takes the one payment.

It is paid once, with no subscription.

Get Pro back on a new phone

  1. Sign in to the phone with the same Google account you bought with.
  2. Open Settings, Pro.
  3. Tap Restore purchase.

Save a backup

  1. Open Settings, Backup.
  2. Choose to save progress to a file, and pick where to put it.

Move to a new phone

  1. On the old phone, save a backup, and copy the file to the new phone.
  2. Install Chess Pass on the new phone.
  3. Open Settings, Backup and load the file.
  4. Open Settings, Pro, Restore purchase if you bought Pro.

Progress is kept out of cloud backups on purpose, so a backup file is the way across.
